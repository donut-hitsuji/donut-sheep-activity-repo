# Neutral MV Visual Vocabulary Image Prompts

All prompts are for generic educational reference images. They must not imply any specific song, artist, character, or music-video treatment. Avoid readable text, logos, brands, famous compositions, narrative props, and repeated character identity.

No people, no logos, no readable text, no story props. Neutral soft studio lighting, clean 16:9 composition, showing how space, levels, entrances, and negative space shape a music-video world. Not a bedroom, not a concert stage, not a specific real location.
